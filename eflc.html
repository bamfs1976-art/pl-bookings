How this desk works

Every number here comes from 2025-26 form. The Championship season 2026-27 starts in mid-August; until it has minutes in it, this is a pre-season book.

The risk score

A player's booking risk is yellows per 90 × 2 + fouls committed per 90. Fouls are the larger term because they are the thing a referee reacts to; yellows are weighted double because they are the outcome being priced. It is the same formula the Premier League desk uses, from the same tested code (assets/core.js), so the two desks cannot drift apart about what risk means.

From risk to a probability

The risk score is mapped through a logistic curve anchored so that the average minutes-weighted player lands on the league's actual base rate — total yellows divided by total player-matches. Fair odds are that probability inverted. They carry no margin, so a bookmaker's price will always be shorter; the gap is his edge, not an error.

The referee ×factor

Choosing an official on the Players tab rescales every probability by his card rate. The factor blends two signals equally: yellows per game against the league average, and cards per foul — which divides out how foul-heavy his fixtures happened to be and is the purer measure of a strict whistle. It is applied on the odds scale and clamped to ×0.75–×1.30, so a strict referee cannot push an already-likely booking to a silly number.

What “basis” means

  • EFLC 2025-26 Championship form. Same division, directly comparable.
  • PL Burnley, West Ham and Wolves — relegated, so last season's record was earned in the Premier League. Flagged because a foul rate against Premier League opponents is not the same evidence as one earned here.
  • L1 Lincoln, Cardiff and Bolton — promoted, so their form is a League One record. The same caution applies in the other direction.
  • NEW No usable record: minutes too few, or no fouls data. Rates are blank rather than zero, because “unknown” and “never fouls” are different claims.

Known limits — read these before betting anything

No live data. The Premier League desk refreshes in-season from the official FPL API. The Championship has no such free feed, so this desk is a static book until it is refreshed. Nothing here knows about injuries, suspensions or today's team news.
  • Suspension thresholds are not shown because they are not confirmed. The EFL uses the same 5/10/15 ladder as the Premier League with the cutoffs moved for a 46-game season, and 10 yellows before match 37 is a two-match ban — but the 5 and 15 rungs need checking against the regulations, so no strip claims them.
  • Six clubs have no club card rate. They were in other divisions last season.
  • Small samples lie. A player with 200 minutes and one foul reads as a high rate. The 450-minute filter is on by default for that reason; turn it off knowing what you are seeing.
  • Fair odds are a model, not a forecast. They exclude the margin every real price carries. Beating them is not the same as beating the bookmaker.

Where the data comes from

  • Players — API-Football, fetched one club at a time so a squad is a squad rather than a page of a league.
  • Referees and club card rates — football-data.co.uk match records, public domain, free, no key. Every Championship match with both sides' cards and fouls.
