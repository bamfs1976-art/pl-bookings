# Layout and sections

> Moved unchanged from the old README.md on 8 September 2026 ("Layout and sections"). The README is now an orientation page; this file keeps the full prose.

## Layout and sections

The desk uses the same app shell as Gameweek Edge: on desktop a fixed sidebar with the brand, a live gameweek strip (current GW and deadline from the FPL feed) and a flat list of five areas — **This Gameweek**, **Desk**, **Fixtures**, **Tracker**, **Guide** — plus a sticky topbar with a breadcrumb. The sidebar used to expand Desk into a sub-list, which put every view in the app in front of you just to move one step sideways; the lateral move now lives on the page, as an underlined **area tab strip** scoped to whatever you are reading (Desk shows Players · Clubs · Referees). On mobile the sidebar becomes an off-canvas drawer behind a hamburger, and a fixed bottom tab bar (one item per area, safe-area aware) handles quick switching, so installed as a PWA it behaves like a native app.

- **This Gameweek** the default landing view, fixture-first. A card per match in the current gameweek, ordered **hottest to coolest** by booking heat, each led by its referee and listing both sides' highest-risk available players banded by a **fixture-aware booking probability** — the season model chance scaled by the assigned referee's card rate (shown inline as the ×factor) and a derby boost. Bands are High (50%+) and Watch (30–50%); a confidence dot marks sample size (minutes). A **My watchlist** card at the top shows your starred players with their next fixture, referee and probability, sorted. A hero strip carries the gameweek number, a deadline countdown ("X to lock") and the model's live track record. **Live card ticker**: once a match kicks off, the card carries a strip showing yellows and reds so far against the fixture's forecast total, the minute, and the split by side — and any candidate who has been booked stops showing a percentage. That last part is the point. The desk is a forecast and it never used to stop being one, so at 4.30pm a card still read "52%" for a player booked in the twentieth minute: every number computed correctly, the sentence false. A settled outcome now replaces the probability rather than annotating it, on the match cards and on the watchlist, and settled names sink to the bottom of the watchlist. Cards come from the official FPL live endpoint (`event/<gw>/live`, the only free feed carrying in-play cards); totals are summed over the bootstrap's elements rather than the baked squads, so a January signing cannot go uncounted. In a double gameweek the feed reports a round total per player that cannot be split between two fixtures, so the strip relabels itself **GW TOTAL** instead of guessing. **Share cards**: every match card exports a per-fixture image, and a **Share gameweek** button exports the whole round's fixtures ranked by heat — both self-contained PNGs for FPL/tipster content. The live ticker is deliberately *not* on them: a shared image is stale the moment it is posted, and a card count is the one number that must not be.
- **Players** (under Desk) two views of the same squad, chosen with a pill above the table: **Season risk** and **All players**. They were two destinations listing the same players with an overlapping half of the columns and a duplicated filter bar; both renderers are unchanged, only which one owns the body. An old `#p=allplayers` link still opens on the all-players view. **Season risk** is a sortable table of every squad player by season-long booking risk, filter by club and position, search, hide low sample. A **P(card)** column turns each risk score into a model-implied chance of a booking with fair decimal odds — click it and paste a bookmaker's price to get an edge % and a value verdict (an estimate, framed honestly in the Guide). Once the season is under way, a small ▲ shows recent card form. Star any player onto a **watchlist** and flip the ★ filter to see just your shortlist. Injury/suspension/doubt flags from the live feed, and a **suspension watch** strip for anyone one booking from a ban (5 yellows to GW19, 10 to GW32, 15 all season). Share card exports the current view (a club's risks, or the league top 10) as a publishable social image.
- **All players** *(a view of the Players panel, not its own destination)* the complete current squad list straight from the live FPL feed — every registered player across all 20 clubs, not just the minutes-qualified baked set. Games played (minutes, starts) and discipline (yellows, reds, per-90) are live from FPL; fouls (committed, and won once harvested) are merged from the baked ScoutingStats data by club + name where a player matches, blank otherwise (FPL carries no fouls). Sortable by any column, filter by club/position/search, "with minutes only" toggle, and a CSV export of the merged list. Needs the live feed; pre-season the minute columns are zeros until 2026-27 kicks off.
- **Screener** *(a third view of the Players panel)* the whole division in one grid, on [Tabulator](https://github.com/olifolkerd/tabulator) — player, team, position, minutes, yellows/90, fouls/90, risk, **venue H/A** (the same player's card chance at home and away, venue factor only) and a **conviction** score, every column sortable, with club and position filters and a **minutes-floor slider**. Virtual rendering keeps ~40 rows in the DOM at 667 players, so the slider stays smooth at full squad depth. Rows under the 450-minute floor are **greyed and badged "low sample", never dropped** — and they sort *below* every qualified row whichever column you sort on, because one minute and one foul is a fouls-per-90 of 90.0, arithmetically true and otherwise top of the division. The slider *can* hide rows; the number it hides is printed beside it. **Why** on any row expands the full working behind that player's card probability: raw rate, shrinkage, minutes share, and each of the venue/referee/opponent factors with where it came from.
- **Clubs** the 20 clubs by cards received per game, with a discipline tier and each club's top booking risk.
- **Referees** 2025-26 officials by yellows per game, plus **fouls per game** and **cards per foul** — the latter divides out how foul-heavy his fixtures are and is the purer strictness signal (it carries its % difference from the league average, and feeds the fixture ×factor alongside yellows/game). Reds and penalties per game — plus **the long view**: the league's 26-season card-inflation trend (1992/93–2017/18, sparkline) and each current official's career rate where the historical record has him, so a strict whistle can be judged against his era.
- **Fixtures** every gameweek's schedule from the FPL API as the same rich fixture cards used on This Gameweek — booking-heat rating, referee control and both sides' banded booking candidates. Recognised **derbies** get a heat boost and a chip. Each fixture has a **Ref** control: assign the official once appointments are published (saved locally under `pl_desk_refs_v1`) and every candidate's probability, plus the heat, scales to his card rate; watchlisted players whose next fixture has a strict ref assigned get flagged above the season table.
- **Tracker** log each pick with odds and stake, settle won, lost or void, see hit-rate, staked, P/L and ROI. Signed in, picks sync to the cloud and merge across devices. An optional **AI review** (ported from Booking Analytics Pro) reads your settled picks and points at what's working — see below.
- **Methodology** *(under Guide)* the model against a naive baseline, walked forward over the completed 2025/26 season and **computed in the page** rather than quoted from a file: Brier score, log loss and a ten-bin calibration table for both, at three thresholds, with the paired difference and its 95% interval. See **Backtest** below for what it currently says.
- **Sources & licences** *(under Guide)* every dataset and its licence — epldata (MIT), the DataHub Premier League mirror (PDDL), openfootball (CC0) — the four MIT libraries vendored into the page, and the sources this desk refuses to take data from.
- **Import** *(behind `?admin=1`)* paste or pick a squad CSV, parsed in the browser with [PapaParse](https://github.com/mholt/PapaParse); columns validated against their aliases, rows under the minutes floor flagged and counted, and a `PL_PLAYERS` block printed for `data/pl_data.js`. The flag hides a tool from readers who have no use for it; it is **not** security, and the view says so — this is a static page with no server and nothing to protect. Also accepts the promoted clubs' fouls-drawn figure by hand, labelled as hand-entered wherever it is used.
- **Guide** the method, the risk formula, the implied-probability mapping, the tiers, the known limits — and a **model track record** card that self-scores the desk each finished gameweek (how many of its top-20 risks actually got carded), stored in this browser. Alongside it, a **Live prediction accuracy** card shows the model **graded in the open** — a scheduled server job logs each gameweek's season P(card) and backfills who was actually booked, so the Brier/log-loss and top-20 hit rate are server-verified and shared across everyone, not just this browser (optional; see below).
